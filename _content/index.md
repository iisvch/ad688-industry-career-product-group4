## Make Your Next Career Step More Focused

Which skills should you strengthen, which roles fit your experience, and what salary information can help you compare opportunities?

This project helps **students and early-career job seekers** explore the **Data Analyst / Data Analytics** pathway within **NAICS 523 — Securities, Commodity Contracts, and Other Financial Investments and Related Activities**.

We connect employer requirements with skill self-assessment, experience, compensation and work arrangements to turn job-posting evidence into a practical career-readiness plan.

## The Most Useful Findings

- **Learn SQL, Python and BI tools first:** they combine substantial employer demand with the main development needs identified in the team's skill assessment.
- **Match the experience requirement:** 26 postings, representing 25 profiles, list 0–2 years of minimum experience.
- **Use salary benchmarks:** the median annual midpoint across 48 distinct salary profiles is about **$92,300**. The salary model remains exploratory.
- **Verify remote and location details:** more than half of the postings have an unknown remote classification, and one job may be advertised in several cities.

The study uses **140 Lightcast postings from May–September 2024**, representing **102 distinct posting profiles**. Salary is available for **86 postings**. These findings describe a selected historical sample, rather than the full or current labor market.
